How it works

Your writing, on the record.

Write as you always do. When you save to chain, the version is sealed with a time and linked to the last one, like a diary nobody can back-date.

Live

Write anywhere

A full editor on the web, and Bitcoin Writer for Mac with native menus and local files.

Live

Every version kept

Each save is encrypted and written to Bitcoin. Go back to any version, or fork a draft into two.

Coming

Publish and get read

Sign a piece in your name with bWalletX, share it to bChatX and bNews, and let readers pay to read.

The idea is simple: what you write should stay yours, with proof of when you wrote it. Free pieces stay free. Paid ones cost what they cost to serve, plus a small premium for the writer. We move each item below to Live only when it works.

Status

What works today, and what's next.

Items move to Live when you can use them. We tick them off as they ship.

  • Live

    A full writing app. Write and format documents in your browser, with tabs and autosave. bitcoin-writer.vercel.app

  • Live

    Bitcoin Writer for Mac, 1.5.0. Apple Silicon and Intel, with native menus and saving to and opening files on your Mac. Download from GitHub

  • Live

    Save to chain. Each save is written to Bitcoin as a new version, with a timestamp and a transaction you can look up. File › Save to Chain

  • Live

    Private by default. Your text is encrypted in the app before it is saved, with a key only your sign-in can open.

  • Live

    See the price first. You get a quote in sats before you pay, and you pay from your HandCash wallet.

  • Live

    Version history. A work tree of every save. Open any old version, or fork a document into two branches. History › Work Tree

  • Live

    Open from chain. Paste a save's transaction ID and the document opens again.

  • Coming

    Sign in with bWalletX. Save and pay through any BRC-100 wallet, bWalletX first. Today the app signs in with HandCash.

  • Coming

    Prove it or Inscribe. Put just a fingerprint of your text on chain to prove it existed, or the full text in the open, with bMintX.

  • Coming

    Signed in your name. Each published piece carries your $name, so readers know who wrote it.

  • Coming

    Pay to read. Free stays free. Paid pieces cost what it costs to serve them plus a small premium, and the money goes to the writer.

  • Coming

    Articles in bChatX and bNews. Share a piece into a room or the news feed, with the paid part unlocked in one tap. bnewsx.com

  • Coming

    Files on bDriveX. Keep drafts, images and attachments in your own storage. bdrivex.com
