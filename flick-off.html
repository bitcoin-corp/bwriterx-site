    Get your export

    Medium

    Settings, then Security and apps, then Download your information. Medium emails you a zip of your posts as HTML files.

    Substack

    Your publication Settings, then Exports, then Create new export. You get posts and subscribers. It has no images, which still sit on Substack's servers.

    WordPress

    Tools, then Export, then All content. You get one .xml file of your posts and pages.

    The rules

    Only your published words

    Drafts, private posts and paid-only Substack posts are left out by default. Putting a paid post on Bitcoin would give it away.

    Subscribers stay private

    Subscriber lists and comments are never read into the plan and never minted.

    Cost price, shown first

    The price is the Bitcoin network fee at 100 sats per kB, plus about 1 kB per post for its title, date and signature. You see it before you sign. What you mint is public and permanent.
